# The SmartStyle Closet

Creating a fashion and styling combination web app based on preference, weather insights, color analysis, and day of week. No more spending hours choosing your next outfit of the day!

Based on the Engineering Design Review I created in 2024, for my Software Engineering & Professional Practice course at The University of Birmingham.

## Problem
Students who live on university campuses are normally given facilities in their accommodation,
such as a kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, and closet. With the never-ending changes in the weather,
students may feel distressed trying to pick a suitable outfit that matches their style and protects
them against weather conditions. Students who feel comfortable in their style and coincide with
the weather are more likely to attend lectures on time and gain more confidence, resulting in a
clearer mind and room for retaining knowledge and succeeding in modules. As a part of the
Student Smart Homes technology, we aim to make sure our technology creates efficiency and
accessibility to student living. University students may consider self-expression a vital part of
their everyday lives. Therefore, SSH technology should contribute to those opportunities.

## Solution
We propose The SmartStyle Closet, a new feature in the SSH App. Once students
move into their accommodation at the start of the term, they can install the SSH App on their
devices. The first thing they’ll be able to do is to inform the software of their style and clothing
preferences, along with the list of places they buy their clothes. This feature will also have a
built-in weather forecast, which will help suggest the best outfits to wear daily. Along with other
combinations of outfits, the student can try. The student can also make suggestions and give the
software feedback on how effective the styling was. Ultimately, easing the process of styling
one’s clothes and contributing to one’s self-expression greatly aligns with our objective of a
successful living environment for students.
